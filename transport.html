Transport Policy & Regulations

  • School will organize transport by Van for children admitted to the school, if available on the particular route.
  • School will not be responsible, for the non-availability of the Van service in certain areas, or on certain routes, and parents must take this into account at the time of admission or at the time of change of residence.
  • For the use of the school Van and to discontinue its use, parents are requested to contact the respective Van Driver and negotiate the fees for their child. The school shall in no way interfere or be responsible for the same.
  • Parents of small children shall accompany the child to the Van stop and help the child to board the Van in the morning and receive the child in the afternoon.
  • School allows the private vans to bring the children to school but with the proper permission only and in any mishaps school is not responsible in whatsoever.

Academic Structure

Curriculum at Don Bosco School, Jabalpur

  • Don Bosco is a Primary School at present affiliated to the Madhya Pradesh Board of Education and subsequently it will be affiliated to CBSE pattern.
  • Don Bosco School, Jabalpur, presently has classes from LKG to Class VII, and plans to expand unto Class XII in the near future.
  • The medium of instruction is English, and every effort is made to teach the students to handle English as a written and spoken language. Students are expected to speak English in the school campus. Hindi is taught as a second language.
  • Special attention is given to develop the student’s personality, initiative, originality and sense of national integration through creative co-curricular activities individually and in groups.
  • Great importance is given to a high standard of morality and discipline.

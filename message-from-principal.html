PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

FR. LIEVENS AIND, SDB

“Do not lament that the times are bad, rather ask yourself what you can do to make them better.”

Dear Students, Staff and Parents,

There is a story about a rich farmer who had a beautiful garden. The garden was so attractive, except for one blemish: it had a huge boulder in the middle of the garden. The people admired the garden except for the huge rock in the center. The gardener grew weary of this big, unattractive stone, so he decided to do something about it.

He went to work on it with hammer and chisel. He chipped away the huge stone until it became a beautiful stone elephant. When he finished, it was gorgeous and surprising. The neighbours rushed in to ask, “How did you ever carve such a marvelous likeness of an elephant?” The farmer answered them, “I just chipped away everything that did not look like an elephant!”

A simple story, yet it has a profound implication in our life. Someone has rightly said, “Education has a transformative power.” Education chips away ignorance, bad habits, and negativity, revealing the magnificent human potential within every young mind.

Don Bosco School Jabalpur was established on 27th January 2022. It was blessed by Rev. Fr. Davis Maniparamben, Provincial of the New Delhi Province. Late Fr. Peter Xalxo, Sdb was the first principal of the school, succeeded by Rev. Fr. Arvind Kujur as the second Principal.

Today, we begin our classes with the morning assembly every day — with prayer, physical exercises, and morning motivation talks. We follow the house system and student clubs to teach co-operation, teamwork, and leadership qualities. Apart from academic classes, we organize cultural programs, poem recitation, storytelling, fancy dress, and drawing competitions to enhance their personality.

The school has immense potential to grow and develop. It creates a beautiful ambient for every student to learn, grow, and develop their intellectual capacity, creating good and honest citizens of our country — which is the ultimate purpose of Don Bosco School.

Pastoral Blessing

“Wishing each of you God's Blessings.
Fr. Lievens Aind, SDB — Principal, Don Bosco School, Kosamghat”

PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

FR. LIEVENS AIND, SDB

“Education acts like a chisel — chipping away everything that is unbecoming to reveal the magnificent masterpiece within.”

A rich farmer once possessed a magnificent garden. In the very center lay a huge, rugged, and unattractive boulder that spoiled the vista. Instead of discarding it, the gardener took a hammer and chisel, laboring day after day until a majestic stone elephant was revealed.

When visitors marveled and asked how he envisioned such perfection, the craftsman smiled: “I did not create the elephant — I simply chipped away everything that did not look like an elephant!”

This allegory captures the heart of our mission at Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur. Every child enters our portals endowed with divine gifts, brilliance, and untapped virtue. Education is that loving chisel — removing ignorance, fear, unrefined habits, and complacency.

Grounded in St. John Bosco’s Preventive System of Reason, Religion, and Loving-Kindness, our committed educators walk beside each student to uncover the brilliant, confident, and empathetic person they are destined to become.

“In every young person, there is a point accessible to good and the first duty of the educator is to find that point, that sensitive chord of the heart, and benefit by it.”

— St. John Bosco
Guiding Principles

Our Commitments to Every Scholar

How our school administration translates Don Bosco's vision into daily classroom and campus excellence.

Commitment 01

Academic Vigour

Rigorous CBSE curriculum delivered through active inquiry, practical science/computer lab experiments, smart digital classrooms, and continuous personal mentoring.

Commitment 02

Character & Virtue

Nurturing moral courage, truthfulness, social conscience, and spiritual depth so our graduates step into the world as dependable leaders and compassionate change-makers.

Commitment 03

Family Atmosphere

Fostering an inclusive, caring environment where every student feels secure, valued, and encouraged through the friendly presence (Assistance) of teachers.

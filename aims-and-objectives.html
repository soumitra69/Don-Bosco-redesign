Aims & Objectives of the School

“At DON BOSCO SCHOOL, Kosamghat, we aim to guide our students to become fully integrated and aware persons, endowed with intellectual and moral, emotional, aesthetic, physical and social skills enabling them to become responsible persons, to triumph over all the challenges of the times.”

The school strictly prohibits any sort of physical punishment and follows the Preventive System, taught and practiced by St. John Bosco himself. This system is based on conviction, prevention of faults, loving-kindness, and reverence for God.

Core Guiding Aims

• Aim at the holistic development of every student.
• Prepare students to recognize their inner potentialities and hidden talents.
• Help students appreciate their life, rich Indian heritage, and cultural values.
• Guide students to excel in studies, virtues, and selfless service to humanity.

Objectives

6 Core Aims of Don Bosco School

Deliberate, holistic educational targets instilled in every student from Kindergarten through Class X.

Objective 01

Intellectual Competence

Fostering critical curiosity, scientific inquiry, digital literacy, and conceptual mastery across all CBSE academic subjects.

Objective 02

Moral & Ethical Integrity

Developing truthfulness, moral courage, personal honesty, and ethical discernment to make right choices in life.

Objective 03

Emotional Maturity

Guiding emotional self-awareness, empathy for others, resilience before setbacks, and mental calmness.

Objective 04

Aesthetic Appreciation

Cultivating a deep appreciation for music, fine arts, drama, literature, and the beauty of natural creation.

Objective 05

Physical Vitality

Instilling habits of physical fitness, sportsmanship, outdoor athletics, and teamwork through our active sports block.

Objective 06

Social Responsibility

Nurturing compassionate social awareness, respect for all communities, civic duty, and care for the underprivileged.

Inaugurated and blessed by Rev. Father Davis Maniparamben, sdb, Provincial of the New Delhi Province on 27th January 2022. Administered by the Salesians of Don Bosco.

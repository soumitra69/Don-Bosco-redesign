Founding Heritage

Our Humble Beginnings & Growth

Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur was born out of the ardent desire of the Salesians of Don Bosco (New Delhi Province) to provide quality, value-based CBSE education to the youth of Jabalpur and its surrounding communities along Mandla Road.

The school building was officially inaugurated and blessed on 27th January 2022 by Rev. Father Davis Maniparamben, SDB, Provincial of the New Delhi Province, in the gracious presence of Salesian fathers, brothers, religious sisters, and distinguished local dignitaries.

From its inception, the school was designed not merely as an academic center, but as a vibrant educational family where every child is loved, guided, and empowered through St. John Bosco’s time-tested Preventive System of Reason, Religion, and Loving-Kindness.

Key Landmarks in Our Journey

From foundational vision to an established modern educational sanctuary.

Phase 01

Vision & Land Acquisition

Securing a quiet, scenic, and pollution-free campus location at Kosamghat on Mandla Road to nurture young learners in a peaceful environment.

Phase 02

Solemn Blessing (2022)

Inaugurated on 27th January 2022 by Provincial Fr. Davis Maniparamben, SDB, marking the formal opening of modern classrooms and laboratories.

Phase 03

CBSE Curriculum & Growth

Expanding from early childhood education to primary and secondary levels, with dedicated smart boards, science lab, computer lab, and library facilities.

History of Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur

The school was inaugurated and blessed by Rev. Father Davis Maniparamben, sdb, Provincial of the New Delhi province on 27th January 2022 in the presence of a number of Salesians, invited religious sisters, and distinguished guests from the locality.

Don Bosco School, Kosamghat is situated on the scenic highway to Kosamghat, Mandla Road, Jabalpur. It is an English medium School for boys and girls owned and run by the Salesians of Don Bosco, New Delhi Province. It is an unaided minority Christian institution, open to the children of all communities with a deep social responsibility — to educate and uplift especially that section of society that is socially and economically weak.

At present, the school has classes from Nursery through Class VII with 10 teaching and 4 non-teaching staff members. Classes begin daily with morning assembly — with prayer, physical exercises, and morning motivation talks. Cultural competitions, poem recitation, storytelling, fancy dress, and drawing competitions enhance every scholar's personality.

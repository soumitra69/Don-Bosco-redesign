Welcome to Don Bosco Family

A Worldwide Network Dedicated to the Young

Don Bosco institutions are part of a global network managed by the Salesians of Don Bosco (SDB), a Roman Catholic religious institute founded by Saint John Bosco in the 19th century in Italy. Don Bosco was an Italian Catholic priest, educator, and writer who dedicated his life whole-heartedly to helping disadvantaged and aspiring youth.

Born on August 16, 1815, in Castelnuovo d'Asti, Italy, Don Bosco founded the Salesians of Don Bosco focused on education and holistic care for young people. Despite being born into a poor family and experiencing severe hardship, he pursued his education and was ordained as a priest in 1841. Today, the Don Bosco presence spans across 134 countries worldwide on all six continents.

In India alone, our presence encompasses over 261 schools, 51 colleges, 192 hostels, 138 technical institutions, a university, and numerous social services aimed at empowering vulnerable humanity. With a rich history in India dating back to 1912 when the first Don Bosco Night School was established in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, we provide quality education and holistic character development.

Our approach is rooted in the Preventive System — emphasizing reason, religion, and loving-kindness — providing a nurturing family environment where each child is cherished, guided, and prepared for life's journey.

Salesian Roots in India

Salesians of Don Bosco in India

Over a century of pioneering academic excellence, vocational training, and youth empowerment in India.

Milestone 01

Pioneering Mission (1906)

The Salesians first arrived in India in 1906, establishing foundations in Thanjavur and Northeast India, which blossomed into hundreds of premier educational centres across the subcontinent.

Milestone 02

New Delhi Province

Don Bosco School Kosamghat is governed by the Salesian Province of New Delhi, committed to bringing high-caliber, value-based CBSE education to Jabalpur and Madhya Pradesh.

Milestone 03

Jabalpur Campus (2022)

Officially inaugurated on 27th January 2022 by Provincial Fr. Davis Maniparamben, SDB, the Kosamghat campus on Mandla Road is a modern beacon of academic vitality and moral discipline.

Inaugurated and blessed by Rev. Father Davis Maniparamben, sdb, Provincial of the New Delhi Province on 27th January 2022. Administered by the Salesians of Don Bosco.

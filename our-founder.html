OUR FOUNDER

SAINT JOHN BOSCO (DON BOSCO)

“Educating the young is a work of the heart.”

Don Bosco bequeathed his rich heritage to the Salesian Society which consists of Priests, Brothers, Sisters, Co-operators, and Collaborators, to continue his educational System across the globe.

Born on August 16, 1815, in Becchi, Italy, John Bosco recognized that young people needed more than formal instruction — they required guidance, affection, safe shelter, vocational mastery, and genuine moral grounding.

Following the footsteps of the great educator Saint John Bosco, Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur strives to offer integral formation to the young through academic discipline, cultural appreciation, character formation, and a welcoming family atmosphere.

“Do you want to do a good deed? Teach the young. Do you want to do a holy thing? Teach the young. Truly, this work is among the most divine.”

— St. John Bosco
Don Bosco's Educational Method

The Preventive System of Education

A compassionate educational methodology developed by Don Bosco, rooted in proactive mentoring rather than punitive discipline.

Pillar 01

Reason (Ragione)

Emphasis on rational dialogue, clear rules explained with patience, intellectual clarity, and mutual understanding. Every regulation makes sense to the student, fostering self-discipline rather than blind obedience.

  • Clear rational guidelines
  • Patient mentorship and dialogue
  • Critical thinking & intellect
Pillar 02

Religion (Religione)

Cultivating love for God, moral conscience, reverence for universal human values, and selfless service to society. We guide every scholar to develop an enlightened ethical compass and deep spiritual tranquility.

  • Moral integrity & sound conscience
  • Reverence for all creation & diversity
  • Service to the less privileged
Pillar 03

Loving-Kindness (Amorevolezza)

It is not enough for the young to be loved — they must know and feel that they are loved. Educators participate in student life with empathy, friendliness, active presence, and cheerful encouragement.

  • Genuine educator presence (Assistance)
  • Warm and joyful family spirit
  • Building lifelong self-confidence
Global Salesian Network

A Worldwide Mission for Youth

Today, Don Bosco’s educational vision lives on in more than 134 countries across six continents. The Salesians of Don Bosco constitute one of the largest Catholic educational organizations in the world, serving millions of young people through schools, technical institutes, universities, and youth centers.

In India, the Salesians oversee thousands of reputed educational institutions. Don Bosco School at Kosamghat, Jabalpur, is governed by the Salesian Province of New Delhi, maintaining the identical international benchmarks of character formation, academic rigor, and compassionate social awareness.

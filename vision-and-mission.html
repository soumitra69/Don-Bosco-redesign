FOUNDATIONAL HORIZON

Vision of Don Bosco School

Integral Formation of Youth
01
“Following the footsteps of the great educator — Saint John Bosco, we the Educational Institutions of Don Bosco in India strive to offer integral formation to the young vis-a-vis education, culture, God-experience, personal and social development and help them to make right choices in life.”

In a family atmosphere of joy and optimism and with the educative system of Don Bosco — Reason, Love of God and Loving kindness — we educate the future leaders of our nation.

Right Choices Ethical discernment in life
Family Spirit Atmosphere of joy & optimism
Future Leaders Nurturing tomorrow's citizens

DAILY MISSION

Mission of Don Bosco School

Kosamghat, Jabalpur
02
“At DON BOSCO SCHOOL, Kosamghat, we aim to guide our students to become fully integrated and aware persons, endowed with intellectual and moral, emotional, aesthetic, physical and social skills enabling them to become responsible persons, to triumph over all the challenges of the times.”

The school strictly prohibits any sort of physical punishment and faithfully follows the Preventive System, based on conviction, prevention of faults, correction, loving kindness, and reverence for God.

Zero Punishment Loving kindness & guidance
Holistic Skills Intellectual, moral & social
Reverence for God Spiritual and ethical grounding

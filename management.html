    Institutional Governance

    Management of Don Bosco School, Jabalpur

    Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur is managed by the Salesians of Don Bosco (Catholic Minority Institution). The school management ensures that Saint John Bosco's time-tested Preventive System of Reason, Religion, and Loving-Kindness remains the cornerstone of daily educational and administrative life.

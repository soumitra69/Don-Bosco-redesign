WITHDRAWAL AND DISMISSAL

Official regulations governing student withdrawal, transfer certificate requirements, code of discipline, and institutional norms at Don Bosco School, Kosamghat.

Institutional Code of Discipline

Rules Governing Withdrawal & Dismissal

Don Bosco School maintains high standards of discipline, commitment, and mutual respect. To ensure order and uphold pedagogical integrity, the school operates under clear withdrawal procedures and disciplinary regulations.

Application for Transfer Certificate

For withdrawal from the school and the Transfer Certificate, the Application Form has to be filled in and signed by the parents.

Clearance of All Dues & Quarter Fees

The current quarter fees must be paid in full before withdrawal, and in like manner all dues to the school by way of activities, must be paid.

Breach of Discipline & Misconduct

Irregular attendance, habitual illness, disobedience, bad conduct, sickness liable to be injurious to other students and serious breach of the rules of discipline justify dismissal.

Unauthorized Absence (15 Calendar Days)

If an unauthorized absence from school exceeds 15 calendar days, the student’s name may be struck off the rolls, and the student may not be readmitted to school.

Right to Suspend or Dismiss

The school reserves to itself the right to suspend or dismiss pupils whose diligence or progress in study is steadily unsatisfactory or whose conduct is harmful to others, or whose parents show no interest in the progress of their wards. No attendance would be given to a student during the period of suspension.

Finality of Principal's Decision

The Principal’s decision is final in all cases of dismissal or withdrawal.

Important Note for Parents: Application for a Transfer Certificate should be made at least one week in advance. All school property including library books and sports equipment must be surrendered, and clearance obtained from all departments before the T.C. is released.

Have questions regarding institutional policies?

Reach out to the School Administrative Office during working hours (9:00 AM – 1:30 PM).

ASSESSMENT AND PROMOTION

Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur • Academic Evaluation, Examination Criteria & Progress Guidelines

Evaluation Standards

Official Assessment & Promotion Policy

  • Promotion at the end of the year will be based on attendance, continuous assessment in every subject work done during the year and overall conduct record.
  • Class tests and assignments are given regularly during each term, and a certain percentage of marks is allotted for them. There will be Unit and Terminal Examinations in the months of July, September, and December, and Final Examination in the month of March. For promotion to the next class, a minimum of 40% marks in each subject is required for all classes.
  • The answer papers of the Class tests and Unit tests may be shown to the parents, but the scripts of the Annual Examination will not under any circumstance be shown to the guardians or private tutors.
  • For students who are absent for any assessment, no provision can be made for supplementary tests.
  • If a student is unable to appear for any examination due to illness, a medical certificate has to be produced to this effect. This will, however, have no claim over promotion. If the absence is justified, the fact will be taken into consideration at the end of the school year.
  • Progress cards issued after Examinations are to be countersigned by the parents and returned to the school on the date notified. Defaulters may be penalized through a fine and may not be allowed to attend school until the report is collected and returned.
  • Regular attendance in class is an essential condition for promotion. A student will not be promoted to the higher class if his/her attendance falls below 90%. Condonation is exercised only in exceptionally valid cases and that too only to the extent of 5% shortage.
  • The breach of any regulations of conduct at examinations or the use of unfair means, even if discovered subsequently, will result in the cancellation of the particular paper, the student concerned will be awarded zero, and he/she can be expelled from the entire examination (that is, all subjects).

Student Grouping & Formation

The Four Houses of Don Bosco School

Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur believes that properly organized and regulated co-curricular activities are just as much a part of sound education as studies. To cultivate leadership, healthy competition, camaraderie, and team spirit, the entire student body is divided into four distinct houses.

House Leadership & Student Council

Captains, vice-captains, and house leaders proudly holding the Red, Green, Yellow, and Blue house flags during the official investiture assembly.

House Council

RED HOUSE

Symbolizes vitality, passion, and courage. Fosters active participation in athletic tournaments, cultural events, and leadership initiatives.

House Colour: Red

GREEN HOUSE

Symbolizes growth, environmental stewardship, and endurance. Emphasizes discipline, nature care, and teamwork across academic and physical forums.

House Colour: Green

YELLOW HOUSE

Symbolizes wisdom, intellectual brilliance, and joy. Encourages scholastic competitions, literary quizzes, debating, and collaborative creative projects.

House Colour: Yellow

BLUE HOUSE

Symbolizes loyalty, truth, and steadfast integrity. Inspires sportsmanship, community service, moral responsibility, and quiet confidence.

House Colour: Blue

Objectives of the House System

  • To channel youthful energies constructively into games, sports, and cultural tournaments.
  • To inculcate a healthy spirit of competition and fair play, where victory is celebrated and defeat is accepted gracefully.
  • To provide every student with an opportunity to exhibit personal talents and develop collective leadership skills.
  • To build lasting fraternal bonds between students across different grade levels under the guidance of appointed House Masters.

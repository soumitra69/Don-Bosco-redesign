    OUR FOUNDER

    ST. JOHN BOSCO

    “In every young person, a point of goodness is accessible and it is the primary duty of the educator to discover that sensitive cord of the heart and bring it out.”

    Don Bosco bequeathed his rich heritage to the Salesian Society which consists of Priests, Brothers, Sisters, Co-operators and Collaborators, to continue his educational System.

    Founded on reason, religion, and loving-kindness (the Preventive System), his pedagogical legacy spans across 130+ nations today, bringing transformative value-based education to every child.

    OUR HISTORY

    DON BOSCO KOSAMGHAT

    “Building on a centennial foundation of educational excellence, sowing seeds of hope and knowledge in Kosamghat.”

    The school was inaugurated and blessed by Rev. Father Davis Maniparamben, sdb, Provincial of the New Delhi province on 27th January 2022 in the presence of a number of Salesians and other religious and guests.

    From day one, the institution has committed itself to providing modern academic infrastructure intertwined with Don Bosco's world-renowned preventive educational philosophy.

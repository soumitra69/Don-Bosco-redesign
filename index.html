Welcome to Our School

A Friendly School, Having Pride in Their Students

At DON BOSCO SCHOOL, Kosamghat, we aim to guide our students to become fully integrated and aware persons, endowed with intellectual, moral, emotional, aesthetic, physical, and social skills enabling them to become responsible persons and triumph over all the challenges of the times.

Vision & Mission of the School

Inspired by St. John Bosco's worldwide educational legacy across 134 nations, we form upright citizens of deep moral integrity, intellectual excellence, and compassionate leadership.

01. Our Vision

Future • Integrity

“To envision well-formed youngsters who are honest citizens and persons of deep moral integrity, intellectual vitality, and compassionate social conscience.”

  • Honest Citizens: Forming upright individuals grounded in truthfulness, civic responsibility, and ethical courage.
  • Preventive System: Educating through Reason, Religion, and Loving Kindness in a joyful, welcoming family atmosphere.
  • Universal Brotherhood: Fostering mutual respect, human dignity, and selfless service across all cultural backgrounds.

02. Our Mission

Purpose • Action

“To prioritize the integral development—spiritual, intellectual, moral, aesthetic, social, and physical—of every student entrusted to our care.”

  • Holistic Scholarship: Combining rigorous academic disciplines with modern digital literacy, science, and the creative arts.
  • Educative Presence: Creating a safe home-away-from-home where teachers actively mentor students with loving accompaniment.
  • Social Compassion: Cultivating environmental stewardship, empathy for the underprivileged, and active community outreach.

The Don Bosco Advantage

Why Should You Choose Don Bosco School?

Choosing the right school for your child is a foundational decision. At Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur, we combine 165+ years of worldwide Salesian pedagogical heritage with modern, value-based holistic education.

134+ Countries with Active Salesian Institutions Worldwide
100% Commitment to Character, Intellect & Child Well-Being
Apply for Admission 2026–27

Our educational philosophy is rooted in Reason, Religion, and Loving Kindness. Unlike punitive systems, Don Bosco’s preventive approach accompanies children with loving mentorship, guiding them to do right out of conviction and joy rather than fear of punishment.

We balance disciplined academic scholarship with strong character formation. Through our four houses (Bosco, Dominic, Laura, Rinaldi), students cultivate empathy, public speaking, leadership skills, and ethical courage.

Located along Mandla Road, Kosamghat, our campus provides a tranquil, distraction-free environment with 24/7 security, dedicated school bus transportation, and faculty members who act as approachable elder guides in a welcoming family atmosphere.

From modern computer laboratories and rich library reading spaces to dedicated athletic grounds, yoga training, environmental green projects, and musical celebrations, every child discovers their unique talents.

As part of the Salesians of Don Bosco (New Delhi Province), a student from Don Bosco belongs to an illustrious global fraternity known worldwide for integrity, competence, and service to society.

OUR FOUNDER

ST. JOHN BOSCO

“Educating the young is a work of the heart.”

Don Bosco bequeathed his rich educational heritage to the Salesian Society which consists of Priests, Brothers, Sisters, and Collaborators, continuing his preventive educational system across 134 countries today.

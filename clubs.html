STUDENT CLUBS & MOVEMENTS

“Fostering intellectual, moral, emotional, aesthetic, physical, and social dimensions.”

Co-Curricular Student Movements

Clubs at Don Bosco School, Jabalpur

Following the Salesian Preventive System, Don Bosco School Kosamghat provides structured student clubs and movements designed to draw out every child's innate potential beyond textbook learning, cultivating creative expression, scientific inquiry, environmental responsibility, and social concern.

Holistic Formation

Student clubs operate under the guidance of teacher coordinators to provide experiential learning opportunities that reinforce character formation, communication skills, and collaborative leadership.

Don Bosco Youth & Club Movement

Students and educators gathered together around the St. John Bosco statue, inspiring character formation and co-curricular excellence.

Youth Movement

Science & Eco Club

Encourages scientific temper through experimental demonstrations, exhibits, nature conservation initiatives, and tree-plantation drives on the school campus.

Literary & Debating Club

Promotes language fluency, creative writing, elocution, recitation, and inter-house debates in English and Hindi to build confidence on stage.

Art, Craft & Cultural Club

Nurtures visual aesthetics, drawing, painting, handmade models, and musical/theatrical preparation for school cultural assemblies and national festivals.

Social Action & Service Movement

In keeping with Don Bosco's mission for youth, sensitizes students to the needs of the underprivileged, elderly, and neighboring rural communities through service drives.

Participation Guidelines

Every student is encouraged to enroll in at least one club per academic year. Club activities take place on designated activity periods and Saturdays, allowing students to balance rigorous scholastic pursuits with creative hobbies.

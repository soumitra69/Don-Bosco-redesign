MANAGER'S MESSAGE

FR. JOSE MATHEW SDB

“At Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur, every child is cherished and education becomes a journey of the heart.”

Dear Parents, Students and Well-Wishers,

It is with great joy and hope that I welcome you to Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur, where every child is cherished and education becomes a journey of the heart. Rooted in the rich educational heritage of Saint John Bosco and part of a global network spanning 134 countries, our school is more than just a place of learning — it is a space of transformation, care, and possibility.

Founded by Saint John Bosco, a visionary 19th-century educator, the Salesian tradition is built on the pillars of reason, kindness, and faith. With over 300 institutions across India, we remain steadfast in our mission: to serve the young — especially those in need — through education that nurtures the whole person: mind, heart, and spirit.

In today’s fast-paced world, education often tends to emphasize performance, comparison, and competition. In the process, the individual child can sometimes get lost, reduced to grades, ranks, and results. At Don Bosco School, we respectfully choose a different path. We believe that education must begin with the heart.

Every child is unique, and we take the time to understand their individual strengths, needs, and dreams. Our goal is not merely to prepare students for exams, but to prepare them for life — with values, courage, compassion, and clarity. At Don Bosco Kosamghat, we create an environment where differences are celebrated, talents are nurtured, and each child is encouraged to become the best version of themselves.

Though we are at the beginning of our journey at Don Bosco School Kosamghat, our vision is clear: we are building a school that is a home away from home — a place where your child is safe, seen, and supported. Here, your child will not be a number, but a name. Not a face in the crowd, but a soul with promise.

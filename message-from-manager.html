MANAGER'S MESSAGE

REV. FR. MANAGER

“It is with great joy and hope that I welcome you to Don Bosco School, Kosamghat, Jabalpur, where every child is cherished and education becomes a journey of the heart.”

Education is the greatest investment a family can make in the future of their child. At Don Bosco School Kosamghat, we are honored to partner with parents in shaping young minds into responsible, capable, and compassionate men and women of character.

As an administrative body, our unwavering focus is on providing modern, safe, and stimulating school infrastructure. From well-equipped digital classrooms and advanced science/computer labs to sprawling sports grounds and safe bus transport across Jabalpur, every facility is thoughtfully maintained to serve our students' growth.

But beyond buildings and facilities, our greatest treasure is our teachers and the Salesian family spirit. In the spirit of Don Bosco, we welcome every parent and scholar into our home, walking with them hand-in-hand toward a brighter future.

“Without confidence and love, there can be no true education. If you want to be loved, you must love; and not only love, but make your children feel that you love them.”

— St. John Bosco

Our Administrative Commitments

Ensuring campus safety, cutting-edge facilities, and transparent parental collaboration.

Focus 01

Safe & Inspiring Campus

Serene, green campus on Mandla Road equipped with CCTV surveillance, clean drinking water, dedicated infirmary, and supervised transport coverage.

Focus 02

Qualified & Caring Faculty

Carefully selected and continuously trained educators who embody the Preventive System, offering individual academic attention to each student.

Focus 03

Parent-School Partnership

Transparent communication through regular PTMs, digital portal notices, and open administrative office hours (9:00 AM – 1:30 PM) for parent support.
